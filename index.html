Song Production: What to Expect

My recent song productions have averaged around $1,800 per song, depending on how many musicians are involved and what the recording requires.

Bring a voice memo, rough demo, existing session, or developed arrangement. We’ll decide what the song needs and agree on the scope before production begins.

For band productions, the final quote depends on:

  • Whether you bring your own band or need hired musicians.
  • The number of players and recording sessions.
  • Arrangement and production needs.
  • Editing, overdubs, and revision requirements.
  • Studio and other outside costs.

Deliverables & Revisions

Proposed flat-rate packages include:

  • Two rounds of consolidated revisions for mixing and production.
  • One round of mastering adjustments.
  • One performance adjustment for a bass-recording package.
  • Final audio files in the formats agreed upon before work begins.

Additional versions, extensive editing, new recordings, arrangement changes after approval, or work beyond the agreed scope are quoted separately.

Software

UPGRADR

UPGRADR is the diagnostic software behind DAW Doctor. It scans a system, isolates where a session is failing, and turns hours of guesswork into a short, prioritized fix list.

  • Driver and hardware conflict detection
  • DAW session health and plugin checks
  • Routing and I/O diagnostic report
  • A clear list of what to fix first

UPGRADR is the tool. DAW Doctor is the service. Recording and production at Clayton Studios is booked on its own.

About Justin

Experience, Credits, and Approach

Justin DiCenzo records, produces, and mixes at Clayton Studios, and works with artists and engineers through DAW Doctor when the studio itself is in the way.

His background is in high-pressure broadcast and film. Work on Anderson .Paak's "Ventura" (Grammy Winner), Get Out, and Arrested Development required the same precision he brings to a song session or a home studio. He teaches at Lindenwood University.

Justin is a Resident Engineer at Clayton Studios, St. Louis' longest-running recording studio since 1976. Recording and production sessions happen there. DAW Doctor sessions happen remotely, or at your studio, with the scope and price agreed before the work begins.
